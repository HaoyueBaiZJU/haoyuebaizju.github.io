Haoyue BAI

Haoyue Bai is a second-year Ph.D. student at Department of Computer Science and Engineering, the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. From 2020 to 2021, she did an internship in Huawei Noah's Ark Lab, AI Theory Group. She obtained her bachelor's degree at Zhejiang University in 2018.

Research

I'm interested in computer vision, machine learning and image processing. Much of my research is about generalization of deep learning algorithms and the applications of machine learning in industrial.

DecAug: Out-of-Distribution Generalization via Decomposed Feature Representation and Semantic Augmentation
Haoyue Bai*, Rui Sun*, Lanqing Hong, Fengwei Zhou, Nanyang Ye, Han-Jia Ye, S.-H. Gary Chan, Zhenguo Li
AAAI, 2021
arXiv / poster / slides / press

Designing a general OoD generalization framework to tackle possible correlation shift and diversity shift in the real world.
Crowd Counting on Images with Scale Variation and Isolated Clusters
Haoyue Bai, Song Wen, S.-H. Gary Chan
ICCVW, 2019
arXiv / code / poster / dataset

Quantifying two dimension crowd counting challenges: large variation in object scale and isolated clusters of objects. We also propose a novel scale-adaptive long-range context-aware network for crowd counting.
A Survey on CNN-based Single Image Crowd Counting: Network Design, Loss Function and Supervisory Signal
Haoyue Bai, S.-H. Gary Chan
under review, ACM Computing Surveys, 2021
arXiv / project page / slides

Providing an up-to-date review of recent crowd counting approaches, and educate new researchers in this field the design principles and trade-offs.
Gradient Orthogonal Decomposition and Semantic Augmentation for Out-of-Distribution Generalization
Haoyue Bai, Rui Sun, Huayu Deng, Kaican Li, Lanqing Hong, Fengwei Zhou, Nanyang Ye, Han-Jia Ye, S.-H. Gary Chan, Zhenguo Li
under review, IJCV, 2021

We extend DecAug with a theoretical justification of gradient orthogonal and show more substantical experimental and visualization results.
OoD-bench: Benchmarking and Understanding Out-of-Distribution Generalization Datasets and Algorithms
Nanyang Ye, Kaican Li, Lanqing Hong, Haoyue Bai, Yiting Chen, Fengwei Zhou, Zhenguo Li
under review, NeurIPS, 2021

The new benchmark may serve as a strong foothold that can be resorted to by future OoD generalization research.
